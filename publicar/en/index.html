Frequently asked questions
Before you book

      

        
Is the house close to the beach?
Yes. The house is about 500 m from Posto 6 beach in Barra da Tijuca, reachable on foot or by ferry across Marapendi Lagoon.

        
What is the difference between the whole house and the Villa?
The whole house includes the main house and the annex, for up to 18 people. The Villa is only the ground-floor annex with its own entrance, for up to 10 people, and the main house stays closed. In both, the leisure area is reserved for your group.

        
Is it in a gated community? Can we have a party?
It is a standalone property with no condominium rules. After 10 pm we ask for background-level music outdoors, out of respect for the neighbors. For louder music, use the living room with the doors closed.

        
How does payment work?
You send your request by WhatsApp, the host confirms availability and price and sends a Mercado Pago payment link. You can pay by Pix or credit card, protected by Mercado Pago. The booking is guaranteed once payment is confirmed.

        
Is there parking?
Yes, private parking for 8 cars.

        
Can I bring my pet?
Yes, pets are welcome. Just mention it in your booking message.
